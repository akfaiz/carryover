# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background, identity or experience. In issues, pull requests and anywhere
else connected to the project:

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; admit mistakes and learn from them.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, personal or political attacks, trolling, or publishing
  someone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

If someone behaves in a way that breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainer,
[akfaiz](https://github.com/akfaiz), through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept
confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues or other contributions that break this code, and may warn,
temporarily ban or permanently ban whoever is responsible, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant.
